# ScienceoLogy | Maths & Science Learning Hub

> An open-access educational web portal offering free chapter notes, video lectures, and timed self-assessments for school students.

**ScienceoLogy** is designed for Class 5 to 10 students preparing under CBSE, ICSE, and WBBSE (Madhyamik) curricula. Mentored by Md Amaan [B.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics, CU], the platform provides single-click access to curated formula sheets, daily quizzes, past examination papers, and timed mock tests without mandatory sign-ups or paywalls.
